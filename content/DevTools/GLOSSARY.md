# Glossary

One line per term, with the official page it comes from and the note that explains it. **All definitions are [Docs] paraphrases.**

Terms are added as each module's notes are written (75 terms, modules 00–08, October 22, 2026). Ontology terms (object type, action type, function) are in the [Ontology glossary](../Ontology/GLOSSARY.md); Workshop and app terms are in the [Apps glossary](../Apps/GLOSSARY.md).
