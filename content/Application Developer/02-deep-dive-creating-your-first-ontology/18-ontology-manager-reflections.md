# Lesson 18 — Ontology Manager Reflections

## Course Essentials
The simple root-cause action demonstrates semantic intent, controlled mutation, and capture of human judgment. A fuller workflow could assign operators, route alerts to maintenance, rebook passengers, change gates, reschedule aircraft, swap aircraft, and resolve cases.

## Design from decisions backward
Start with decisions users must make, then identify evidence required, actions permitted, and feedback produced. This avoids creating a data catalog with no operational purpose.

As decisions expand, new concepts emerge: crew availability, aircraft availability, passenger impact, assignment, and resolution. Workshop can provide the tailored interface while reusing ontology objects, links, and actions.

## Deeper Mastery
The “digital twin” is not merely a mirror. It combines observed state, relationships, predictions, constraints, and permitted interventions. Its quality depends on freshness, fidelity, governance, uncertainty handling, and feedback loops.

## Deep Understanding Check
Choose one proposed action and list the objects, links, constraints, permissions, and failure modes it requires.
