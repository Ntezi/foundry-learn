# Lesson 20 — Summary

Official lesson: https://learn.palantir.com/deep-dive-building-your-first-application/1944318

## Course synthesis

The Shipment Operations Center illustrates the operational-application loop:

1. Scope ontology objects with typed variables.
2. Present aggregate patterns and object-level details through widgets.
3. Connect filters, charts, tables, and selection using variables.
4. Use layout, headers, icons, and conditional formatting to clarify intent.
5. Capture decisions through point-and-click or function-backed actions.
6. Publish immutable Workshop versions for operational users.

The design begins with business decisions rather than widgets. Charts and tables supply evidence, the Gantt view makes candidates discoverable, and actions turn a user's judgment into shared ontology state.
