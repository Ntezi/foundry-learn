# Lesson 17 — Actions and Decision Making Overview

Official lesson: https://learn.palantir.com/deep-dive-building-your-first-application/1944314

## From insight to decision

The module already exposes departure, arrival, quantity, and vehicle capacity. The next requirement is durable decision capture. Writing the decision to the Ontology lets other users and workflows approve it, forecast around it, reuse spare capacity, or synchronize it to an external schedule system.

The course first implements a simple single-shipment action, then a function-backed action for multiple shipments. The split demonstrates the selection rule: use point-and-click effects for straightforward mappings; use Functions when iteration, conditions, or more complex logic are required.
