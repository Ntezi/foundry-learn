# Glossary

One line per term, with the official page it comes from and the note that explains it. **All definitions are [Docs] paraphrases.**

Terms are added as each module's notes are written (59 terms after modules 01–07, October 18, 2026). Ontology terms (object type, action type, function) are in the [Ontology glossary](../Ontology/GLOSSARY.md); AI terms are in the [AIP glossary](../AIP/GLOSSARY.md).

|---|---|---|---|
