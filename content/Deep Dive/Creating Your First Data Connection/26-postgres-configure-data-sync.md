# 26 — Configure a Data Sync

*Module: Data Connection: Relational Database · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review · Practice: Not Started*

## Steps

1. **Explore** the source.
2. Click the `plants` table to preview it, then **+** (same as "Add dataset to sync").
3. **Create sync for 1 dataset**, open build details, and wait for success.

The workflow is identical to S3: source → explore → sync → build. What changes is the connector and what you browse (tables instead of files).
