## Walkthrough
**[Course]** Star the Project, then **New → Folder**, named after the course (for example *Deep Dive: Building your first Pipeline*).

**[Explanation]** The pipeline's outputs will be created **in the pipeline's folder** when you deploy ([lesson 16](16-add-a-dataset-output.md)). A dedicated folder keeps the pipeline, its three raw inputs and its outputs together, which makes lineage easy to read.
