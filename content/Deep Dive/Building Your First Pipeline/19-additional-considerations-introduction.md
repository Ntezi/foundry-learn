## What this module covers
**[Course]** Real data work is messier than the exercise. This module introduces more Pipeline Builder capabilities, **streaming** and **outputting to the Ontology**, and best practices: **UDFs** for cases pre-built transforms can't handle, **segmenting** and choosing what to **materialize**, and **long-term maintenance**.

**[Explanation]** These lessons are conceptual. There are no hands-on steps, so the practice plan treats them as discussion and design exercises.
