# Shapes for Ontology notes

There is no single template. Pick the shape that fits the subject, or combine two. Different subjects are learned differently: a screen is best toured, a list of rules is best scanned, a procedure is best followed, a trade-off is best decided.

## Every note has

- The title: `# NN — Title`.
- One line under it: `*Sources: [page](url) · [page](url) · checked Month D, YYYY · Status*`.
- Labels — **[Docs]**, **[Concept]**, **[Analogy]**, **[Explanation]**, **[Recommendation]**, **[Example]**, **[Observed]** — wherever a reader could mistake an interpretation for documented behaviour. Not on every line.
- Navigation at the bottom: `[Module index](README.md) · Previous · Next`.
- Open questions as `**Open question** [RQ-NN](../SOURCES.md#open-research-questions)`.

Self-check questions go in [18 — Self-check](../18-practice/03-review-questions-by-module.md), not at the end of each note.

## Useful sections, when they earn their place

The topic, how Foundry implements it, a Kestrel example, a comparison with familiar tools, trade-offs, mistakes, caveats, links to our course notes. Name each section after its content ("Kestrel: Priya picks keys"), not after its role ("Worked example").
