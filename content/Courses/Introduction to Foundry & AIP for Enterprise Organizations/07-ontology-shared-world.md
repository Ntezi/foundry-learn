# 07 — The Ontology as the Shared World for Human + AI Teams

*Module: Section 1: The Centerpiece - The Ontology · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Why "shared world" matters

This is what the course says separates the Ontology from a traditional warehouse or semantic layer. LLMs were not trained on your business's data or processes, so for AI to be useful it needs **context**, and the Ontology supplies it. Building on the Ontology gives humans and AI one shared foundation.

## Four audiences at once

- **Humans:** business terms instead of system internals. A plant manager sees "Warehouse X, 42 days of supply" without knowing which SAP table holds it.
- **AI:** the context to reason. Not raw numbers, but that a warehouse is low, which plants depend on it, the lead-time constraint, and which actions can fix it.
- **Systems:** a single source of truth instead of fragmentation across many disconnected enterprise applications.
- **Governance:** visibility into what data exists, who can access it, and what actions are being taken by whom, or by what.

**Remember:** the same Ontology serves humans, AI, systems and governance at the same time.

**Review question:** what does an LLM gain from Ontology access that raw data doesn't give it? Relationships, constraints and available actions, meaning business context it was never trained on.
