# 06 — What is the Ontology?

*Module: Section 1: The Centerpiece - The Ontology · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## The one thing to remember

The course says plainly that if you remember one thing, it should be that **the Ontology is the centerpiece of the whole platform**.

## Nouns and verbs of the business

The Ontology is the nouns and verbs of your business. In manufacturing, plants, warehouses, suppliers, customers, shipments and orders. In healthcare, patients, providers, appointments, prescriptions and equipment. It models how the business really works, **not how IT systems need data structured** to function.

## A semantic layer

It is a semantic layer between raw source-system data and the business language people use. It turns complex, multi-system data into familiar terms so it is accessible and actionable for everyone, from the pipeline engineer to a plant manager making a staffing decision in minutes.

## Engineering comparison

If you know domain-driven design, this is close to a shared domain model with a ubiquitous language, except it is backed by live data and exposes actions, not just types.

**Remember:** objects = nouns, actions = verbs; modeled on the real world, not on source tables.

**Review question:** how does the Ontology differ from simply exposing source tables? It maps data into business concepts people and AI recognize, independent of how each source system stores it.
