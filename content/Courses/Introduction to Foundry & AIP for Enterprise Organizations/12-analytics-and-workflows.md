# 12 — 3.1 Analytics & Workflows

*Module: Section 3: North of the Ontology · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Visibility comes first

The first thing most organizations build on the Ontology is visibility: analytics and workflows where people see the business reflected in data and start making decisions.

## Workflows cross functions

Workflows connect people and processes across business functions instead of silos. One workflow might span supplier monitoring, inventory allocation, production scheduling and customer delivery, showing each owner what they need at their step.

## Live, not static

Because they are built on the Ontology, dashboards reflect the **live state of business objects**, including logic and computed properties, rather than static warehouse reports. Foundry supports **low-code, no-code and pro-code** building.

## Analytics as a byproduct

In traditional BI, the report is the end product and you hope someone acts on it. In Foundry, **analytics are a byproduct of the workflows** you build: insights come out of operational processes, and you can act on them immediately in the same environment.

**Remember:** BI = report, then hope; Foundry = workflow, with the insight and the action in one place.

**Review question:** how does the course contrast Foundry analytics with traditional BI? BI analytics are the end goal; in Foundry they emerge from workflows and can be acted on in place.
