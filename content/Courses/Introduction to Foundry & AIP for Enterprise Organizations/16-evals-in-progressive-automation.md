# 16 — The Role of Evals in Progressive Automation

*Module: Section 4: The AI Infrastructure Stack · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Autonomy needs evidence

Moving a workflow from human-led to AI-led can't be done on faith. You must show that AI recommendations match human-quality decisions often enough, across enough conditions.

## Evals as the evidence base

**AIP Evals** provide the evidence for expanding AI autonomy. Before each step up, teams run evaluation suites against defined criteria. As workflows mature, **continuous monitoring and benchmarking** check that performance doesn't degrade.

## Strategy + evidence

**Progressive automation** is the strategy; **Evals** are the evidence mechanism. Together they give a principled, defensible path from human-led to increasingly autonomous operations, at a pace suited to each use case.

## Note on "phases"

The lesson refers to "each phase transition" but doesn't list the phases in text or diagram. Read it as the gradual shift from human-led toward AI-led work described across the course, not a named, fixed set of stages.

**Remember:** don't expand AI autonomy without eval evidence, and keep monitoring after you do.

**Review question:** what role do Evals play before giving an agent more autonomy? They measure its performance against defined criteria to justify the change.
