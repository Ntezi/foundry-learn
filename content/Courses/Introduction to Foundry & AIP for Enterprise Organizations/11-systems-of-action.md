# 11 — 2.3 Systems of Action

*Module: Section 2: South of the Ontology · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Decisions must land somewhere

Data and logic only matter if they lead to action. **Systems of Action** are the enterprise systems where decisions get written back: ERP, SCM, MES, scheduling, edge (per the course diagram).

## Worked example from the course

The Ontology shows Warehouse A running low on a critical component, and a forecast predicts demand will spike next week. Possible actions: create a stock transfer order, speed up the next shipment, or change the production schedule. Each must land in the right enterprise system.

## Governed and auditable

Actions are **governed** (permissions control who *or what* can run them) and **auditable** (you can always see what happened and why).

**Remember:** writeback to systems of record is how the platform closes the loop and avoids the swivel-chair.

**Review question:** why does the course stress "who *or what*" can run an action? Because AI agents run actions too, under the same permissions and audit as people.
