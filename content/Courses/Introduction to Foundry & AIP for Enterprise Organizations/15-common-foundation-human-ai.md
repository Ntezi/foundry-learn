# 15 — Common Foundation for Human + AI Collaboration

*Module: Section 4: The AI Infrastructure Stack · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Human AND AI, not human OR AI

The core insight: every workflow mixes human effort, AI labor and traditional computation. The question is the right **composition**, not a choice between people and AI.

## What the Ontology provides

It is the shared world where humans see the business in familiar terms, AI agents get the context to reason, every action is governed and auditable, and the human/AI mix can change **without breaking the underlying architecture**.

## Composability in practice

Because both sides use the same data, logic and governed actions, you can adjust the mix fluidly. The course diagram labels the top layer "AI + Human Teaming": models (multimodal, open source, commercial) with operators, developers and analysts.

**Remember:** composability = changing who does a step (human, AI or computation) without rebuilding the system.

**Review question:** why can the human/AI balance change without re-architecture? Both already work through the same Ontology data, logic and actions.
