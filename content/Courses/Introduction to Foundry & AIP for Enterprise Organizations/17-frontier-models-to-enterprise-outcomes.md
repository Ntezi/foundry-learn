# 17 — From Frontier Models to Enterprise Outcomes

*Module: Section 4: The AI Infrastructure Stack · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## The gap

A frontier model can write text, reason about code and analyze images. An enterprise needs it to also understand its business context, reach proprietary data, respect governance, integrate with existing systems and take real-world actions. The course calls the gap between the two enormous; Foundry and AIP are positioned to bridge it.

## The memorable line

The course sums it up with a metaphor: the frontier model is the agent's **brain**, and the Ontology is its **body**.

**Remember:** model = reasoning; Ontology = context, tools, permissions and the ability to act.

**Review question:** in the brain/body metaphor, what does the "body" give the model? Business context, data, governed actions and integration with the real systems.
