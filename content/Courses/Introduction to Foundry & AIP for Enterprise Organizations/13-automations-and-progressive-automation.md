# 13 — 3.2 Automations & Progressive Automation

*Module: Section 3: North of the Ontology · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Automations = generative AI + Ontology context

Automations combine generative AI with the full context of the Ontology to create governed workflows that reason about the business and act on it.

## Three patterns the course names

1. **Reasoning with business logic.** LLMs call **deterministic models** as part of their reasoning. Example: an LLM reads an unstructured maintenance report, then calls a deterministic failure-prediction model for a risk score. The LLM isn't working alone; it orchestrates AI, rule-based, model-driven and human logic, all unified through the Ontology.
2. **Orchestrating complex actions.** Once a decision process is captured in the Ontology, agents can be **embedded directly in the business process**, using the same logic and governed action channels a human operator would.
3. **AIP Evals for confidence.** Because agents act in real systems, observability isn't optional. Evals give a structured environment to test AI-powered functions and agents, with observability over workflows and automations.

**Remember:** LLM for language and judgment, plus deterministic tools for precision; agents inside the process, not beside it; Evals as the trust foundation.

**Review question:** why have an LLM call a failure-prediction model instead of estimating risk itself? The deterministic model is precise and consistent; the LLM contributes the language understanding.
