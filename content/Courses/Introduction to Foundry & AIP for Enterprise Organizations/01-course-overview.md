# 01 — Course Overview

*Module: Overview · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## What this course is for

A 15-minute conceptual course on the "what and why" of Foundry and AIP: the platform's mission, its architecture, and how it turns enterprise data into operational decisions. It is written for every audience, from data engineers to front-line operators to leaders setting AI strategy, and it is the conceptual starting point before hands-on training.

The course says its goal is to explain what makes Foundry different from traditional data platforms and how the components work together to **close the loop between data, decisions and actions**. Keep that phrase in mind: almost every later lesson is a variation on it.

## Disclaimer worth remembering

The course is aimed at employees of organizations that have bought Foundry and/or AIP. It warns that your organization may use Foundry differently, and that some features described may not be available to you yet. So treat the course as a map of platform capabilities, not a description of your own enrollment.

**Remember:** this is the "theory" course of the Aware curriculum. Expect exam questions about *why* the platform is built this way, not about clicks.

**Review question:** what three things does the course say the platform connects in a loop? Data, decisions and actions.
