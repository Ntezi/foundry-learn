# 02 — The Enterprise Autonomy Vision

*Module: Course Introduction - Why Foundry & AIP? · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Maximally ambitious, not minimally viable

The course opens with the ambition behind the platform: an enterprise where very large numbers of AI agents use very large numbers of specific tools across every workflow. It presents this as an engineering target that shapes today's product, not a distant aspiration.

It grounds that in real deployments with messy data and little margin for error: hospital staff scheduling, insurance underwriting, shipyard build operations, and defense and intelligence work.

## Forward-deployed product development

Palantir describes its approach as **forward-deployed**: start from the hardest problems customers actually face and let those define what gets built. The emphasis is on the **outcome**, not the technology.

## Why it matters for the exam

The Aware exam checks that you understand Foundry as a platform *and* how it relates to users, the business and IT. This lesson supplies the "why": the platform is judged by operational outcomes in demanding settings.

**Remember:** outcome orientation and forward deployment are the stated philosophy.

**Review question:** what does "forward-deployed" mean here? Building by working backwards from customers' hardest real problems, with the outcome as the priority.
