# 08 — The Three Pillars of Decision-Centric Systems for Enterprise Automation

*Module: Section 1: The Centerpiece - The Ontology · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Data, logic, action

Every business decision needs three things, and the Ontology brings all three together for human and AI teams:

| Pillar | Question it answers | What the course includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Data** | what IS | All modalities (structured, unstructured, time series, geospatial), unified into business objects people and agents can query |
| **Logic** | how to THINK about it | Business rules, ML models, optimizers and traditional compute, modeled so humans and agents call the same reasoning tools |
| **Action** | what you can DO | Real-world changes (create an SAP order, adjust a schedule, flag an issue), modeled with governance guardrails |

## Plus common security and governance

The course adds a shared security and governance framework that goes beyond role-based access control: **purpose-based controls, data markings, classifications and comprehensive auditing**, spanning human and AI teams.

## Composability

Because humans and AI see the same data, use the same logic and act through the same governed channels, you can **dial the division of labor up or down** between them smoothly.

**Remember:** data = IS, logic = THINK, action = DO.

**Review question:** why does modeling logic *in* the Ontology matter for AI? Agents and humans then use the same reasoning tools instead of the AI inventing its own.
