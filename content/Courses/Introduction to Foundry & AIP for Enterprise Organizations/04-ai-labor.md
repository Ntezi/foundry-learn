# 04 — AI Labor: A New Category

*Module: Course Introduction - Why Foundry & AIP? · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Three kinds of effort

Organizations have traditionally had two kinds of effort: **human effort and thought**, and **traditional computation** (databases, rule engines, scheduled jobs). The course says AI adds a third: **AI Labor**. It is not just faster computation and not a replacement for human judgment; it is a distinct category that bridges the two.

## Finding the balance

Building AI-powered operations means deciding, for each step of a workflow, the right mix of human effort, AI labor and traditional computation. The course calls this a rigorous kind of "alchemy". The mix is not fixed: it shifts as you learn what works, give agents better tools and context, and gain confidence in reliability.

## The design principle

The platform is meant to let these three be **composed, recomposed and progressively automated** on one shared foundation: the Ontology.

**Remember:** human effort + AI labor + traditional computation, mixed per workflow step, with the mix changing over time.

**Review question:** why doesn't the course treat AI as "just faster computation"? Because it is a separate category that sits between human judgment and deterministic computation, and it has to be balanced against both.
