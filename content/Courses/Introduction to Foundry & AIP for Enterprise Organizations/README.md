Updated September 23, 2026. **All 22 lesson notes are prepared and Ready for Review.** The lesson bodies were read from the signed-in course. Optional Course Feedback was not opened. This is one of the two theoretical courses in the Foundry & AIP Aware curriculum, so there is no hands-on practice.

The central question: why is Foundry built around the Ontology, and how does that let data, decisions and actions form one loop that humans and AI share? The course advertises 15–30 minutes. Careful study of the notes takes longer.

Start with the [review guide](REVIEW.md) after reading the lessons. See [sources and corrections](SOURCES-AND-CORRECTIONS.md) for what was inspected and where the course is vague.
