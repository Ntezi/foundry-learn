# Conceptual courses

Notes for Palantir Learn courses that aren't Speedruns or Deep Dives. These are the two theoretical courses in the [Foundry & AIP Aware](https://learn.palantir.com/foundry-aip-aware) curriculum. Each folder has a README, numbered lesson notes, a review guide, sources and corrections, and a lesson manifest.

| Course | What you will learn |
|---|---|
| [Introduction to Foundry & AIP for Enterprise Organizations](Introduction%20to%20Foundry%20%26%20AIP%20for%20Enterprise%20Organizations/README.md) | Why the platform is built around the Ontology; data, logic and action; what sits south and north of the Ontology; progressive automation with Evals; security, branching and Apollo. |
| [Scoping Use Cases for Foundry & AIP](Scoping%20Use%20Cases%20for%20Foundry%20%26%20AIP/README.md) | How to prioritize use cases, prepare a kickoff, avoid scoping pitfalls and scope a workflow block by block. |
