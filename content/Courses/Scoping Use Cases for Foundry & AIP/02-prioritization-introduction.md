## The goal of prioritization

Take every business or team problem that has some link to data, and use a set of criteria (the course calls them "dots") to **rank** them. Working through the criteria should also help you **drop** some candidates, **refine** others, and **merge** a few.

## Three essential areas

The list can be longer for your organization, but these three always come up when deciding whether a use case goes ahead:
