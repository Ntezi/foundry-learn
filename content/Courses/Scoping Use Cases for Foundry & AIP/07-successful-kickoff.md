# 07 — Items for a Successful Use Case Kick Off

*Module: Use Case Preparation Steps · Inspected September 23, 2026 · Notes: Ready for Review*

## Before kickoff

- A **steering committee cadence** is set, with the right attendees to remove blockers and make decisions.
- **Beta users** are identified and have time for fast iteration and testing.
- A **focal point** is named for exploring and integrating data sources.
- A **positive data assessment** is done or under way.
- Approval to **access** the data sources is granted.
- Approval to **connect and ingest** into the target solution is granted.
- The **end-to-end user workflow and interface needs** are defined through **desk-sides and walkthroughs**:
  - demos of users' current systems, processes and tools
  - inefficiencies, bottlenecks and where the new solution has most impact
  - pain points and areas for automation
  - expectations of how the process will change
  - integration logic and business rules, agreed with SMEs and the Product Owner
  - access control rules and user permissions

## Project gates

- Break the use case into **phases and milestones**.
- **Process gates:** What is the **MVP**? What is the **checklist for moving to production**?
- Create **accountability**: assigned project-management roles and clear goals, reviewed regularly. This lets you **stop** projects that aren't mature, are blocked for now, or were picked by mistake, and makes progress reporting easy.

## Keep in mind (shouldn't block kickoff): post-go-live

- **User training**, which also shows what documentation is needed
- A **go-live strategy** agreed with all stakeholders
- A long-term **maintenance plan** and handovers

**Remember:** sponsors, beta users, data focal point, data approvals, desk-sides, gates (MVP → production checklist).

**Review question:** why define process gates? To measure progress against clear goals and to stop immature or blocked projects early.
