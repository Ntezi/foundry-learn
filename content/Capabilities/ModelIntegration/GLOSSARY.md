# Glossary

One line per term, with the official page it comes from and the note that explains it. **All definitions are [Docs] paraphrases.**

Seeded on October 23, 2026 from the nine *Core concepts* pages (overview, getting started, models, Model Studio, objectives, experiments, evaluations, functions on models, selecting the right modeling tool); terms are added as each module's notes are written (80 terms, modules 00–06 written, October 23, 2026). Language-model terms are in the [AIP glossary](../AIP/GLOSSARY.md); compute-module terms in the [DevTools glossary](../DevTools/GLOSSARY.md); live-deployment monitoring terms in the [Observability glossary](../Observability/GLOSSARY.md).
